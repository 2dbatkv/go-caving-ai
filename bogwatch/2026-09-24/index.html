About this work

Three ground rules

01

Public only

Every document came from a public web address — no login, no account, no cookies. Member-only material is excluded and handled separately.

02

Descriptive

What the records say, and what they do not. No motives are assigned. Where a record is a draft, it is labelled a draft.

03

Traceable

Every file has a source address, a SHA-256 fingerprint and a retrieval time recorded in a manifest.

106 Board meetings have minutes that anyone can read today, running from August 1996 to July 2026. 17 of them were special meetings. Counting every version that was posted, including drafts and re-uploads, there are 115 minutes files.

Derivation: meetings and versions counted from the meeting list built from page 1 of every public minutes file, one entry per meeting; agendas counted as doc_type = agenda in the document index. Files collected 22–24 Sep 2026.

The record

Meetings with public minutes, by year

Timeliness

Days from meeting to the first public copy

The Society's own rules set a 30-day expectation for circulating minutes. Measuring from each meeting date to the earliest date any copy of its minutes appeared on caves.org, 3 of 24 meetings since March 2022 fall within 30 days. The median gap is 93 days; the range runs from 5 to 504.
