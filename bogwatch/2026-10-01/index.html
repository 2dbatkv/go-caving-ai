From this call

A question about Article III of the NSS Constitution

Article III of the Constitution used to say the Board of Governors may “appoint officers for the Society.” Since the Board Manual of 1 July 2024 it reads “appoint Executive Director for the Society.” This short document asks a single question: did the members vote on that change?

Article VIII sets out how the Constitution may be amended — proposed by the Board or by petition, mailed to the members, and adopted by a two-thirds majority of the ballots returned within ninety days. That is how Article IX was amended in 2014, carried 1,839 to 135 and recorded in the Board minutes.

For the Article III change, no ballot notice, no vote count and no entry in the Manual's own amendment record has been found in the public record. The document sets out what was searched, what was found, and what was not — including what could not be checked, since NSS News is members-only.
