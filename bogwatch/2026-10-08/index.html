What the call covered

Two questions, one set of ground rules

Part 1 — the Article III question

The rule for amending the Constitution, the wording before and after July 2024, the last time an article was amended by ballot, and what the public record does and does not contain for the Article III change. This continues the question raised on 1 October.

Part 2 — the public financial record

What has been published and when: assets, revenue, audits, the FY2022 gap, the Form 990 filings and their timeliness, who keeps the books and who controls the accounts, recent spending decisions, the executive search, and who held office when.

The deck closes with its limits and its sources — what could not be checked, and every file with its address.
