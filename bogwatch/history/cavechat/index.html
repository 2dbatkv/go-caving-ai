CaveChat, 2005–2026

The NSS discussion forum: founded 2005, retired on paper 2021, online until March 2025, its domain moved in September 2026. What the public record holds, and what it does not say.

Not affiliated with the NSS. A historical account built only from public records: NSS Board documents, the Internet Archive's capture index, archived front pages and domain registries. No forum post, member profile or reader comment was read, and none is reproduced.

Compiled: 2026-10-09. Decisions followed: docs/2026-10-09_cavechat-wayback_questions.md (Q1 answered; Q2 answered "no": no archived topic, profile or member pages are read, and no usernames appear in this account or its data files). Ground rules: Public only · Descriptive · Traceable · No member content.

Three sources, none of which contain members' posts: (1) the NSS Board of Governors minutes, agendas and officer reports already in this archive (index/minutes.sqlite); (2) the Internet Archive's public CDX index of captures (data/cavechat_cdx.jsonl, 782,179 rows: dates, URLs, status codes and sizes only); (3) twenty archived copies of the forum's own front page, roughly one per year (raw/cavechat/, data/cavechat_manifest.csv), which show section names and the running totals phpBB prints for all visitors. The domain-registration record is from the .org registry's RDAP service (data/cavechat_rdap.json). Derived tables: data/cavechat_timeseries.csv, data/cavechat_sections.csv.

Bottom line

  1. CaveChat was the NSS's official discussion forum from 1 September 2005 until it went dark on or about 28 March 2025. It was never hosted at cavechat.org: that name was registered on 3 May 2005 and only ever forwarded to the real forum, first at caves.org/phpBB2/ and from 2007 at forums.caves.org, on NSS infrastructure.
  2. It grew fast and faded slowly. About 15,000–17,000 posts a year from 2007 to 2011; under 7,000 by 2013; under 1,000 by 2017; a few dozen a year from 2021. Registrations never stopped (8,732 accounts by January 2025), but the last recognisably human activity is December 2024.
  3. The Board retired it on paper in March 2021 and it ran for four more years. Act 66-764 was rescinded on the consent agenda; the agenda called the forum "unmaintained and unmanaged for some time" and said it would be "archived by the IT Division and retired", with the archive "made available to NSS members". No later public document reports that archive. The Manual (2022, 2023) and the OVP directory (to March 2024) continued to list the forum and its vacant administrator.
  4. The only complete archive in public view is the Internet Archive's. On 3–4 January 2021, two months before the motion, 334,000 captures were made in two days (304,000 topic pages, 24,000 posting pages, 6,500 member-list pages, 1,000 attachments). Who initiated it is not recorded.
  5. The domain changed hands on 28 September 2026. It was transferred to Amazon Registrar, its nameservers set to AWS Route 53, and its expiry extended to May 2028. The registrant is withheld. Until June 2026 it still forwarded to the dead forums.caves.org host.

1. Before CaveChat (2002–2005)

  • An "NSS Discussion Board" existed at caves.org/soapbox/ by 2002: the site navigation lists "Discussion Board" (Convention Manual page, 2002-12-20; policy pages 2003), and Board motions and agendas were posted to it as they developed (finance FAQ, 2004-06-20 and 2005-04-03; 0411BOG.pdf p.8, where S. Faulkner "encouraged participation").
  • The January 2005 NSS News president's column describes it: announcements, questions for Board members, expeditions, techniques, conservation, photography, education, "gear swap and humor sections" (jan05.pdf).
  • 2005 member survey, one respondent: "The NSS should be careful not to listen to too much of the fluff on the NSS discussion board, as this is not a representative view of the membership" (2005 survey.pdf p.77).

2. Founding and formalization (2005)

  • 3 May 2005: cavechat.org registered (RDAP registration event).
  • 1 September 2005: the phpBB forum's own counter starts ("posts since Sept. 1, 2005", front page of 2007-09-21).
  • July 2005 (0507BOG.pdf p.3): C. Jones moved "The NSS shall maintain a caver's discussion board on the NSS Web site. NSS membership shall not be required to read the board posts or to register." Referred to the OVP.
  • November 2005 (0511BOG.pdf p.3, Rea): adopted. The Discussion Board becomes a subcommittee of the IT Committee; its Administrator is appointed by and reports to the OVP; the IT chair is "responsible only for technical advice and software maintenance"; the Administrator and OVP set the Terms of Service; the Administrator appoints moderators and creates or removes forums. This became Act 66-764 (NSS Discussion Forum).
  • First Wayback capture: 9 February 2006, www.cavechat.org, a frameset page titled "The discussion board of the National Speleological Society" whose frame loads www.caves.org/phpBB2/.

* The August 2008 OVP report's "5,500 posts" is inconsistent with the front page two months later (52,763) and with the 2007 figure; it is probably a transcription slip for ~50,500.

  • Hosting: forums.caves.org answers from July 2007; cavechat.org forwards to it (302) from then on. Captures of the forum's own robots.txt show no file at all (404) for most of 2007–2021, a brief Disallow: / in February 2007, and otherwise the standard phpBB exclusions (posting, search, login, profile, memberlist, private messages).
  • Administration: a Discussion Board contact is listed in the 2007 Manual; a new administrator takes over the back end in 2008 (OVP.pdf, Aug 2008 p.7) and is listed as administrator, NSS Forum Sub-Committee, from 2010.
  • What it was used for, per the Board's reports: event and convention notices and NSS announcements; "information on White Nose Syndrome and new products for caving" (Aug 2008); the WNS Liaison distributing research updates (AVP 2009); the ACA soliciting incident reports; committee chairs posting reports (CFC appeal, 2009); sub-forums requested by grottos (2009: "several"); a private, secure forum space for committee discussions replacing Yahoo groups (ICL report); the Education Division and S&TC planning to use it for outreach (2014).
  • Operations: a CaveChat-specific Terms of Service added (Mar 2009); 279 new members Nov 2009–Mar 2010 and 575 in 2009; phpBB upgraded (Apr 2011) and reinstalled fresh after "several problems" (Jul 2011); a 15-post minimum before selling in the Marketplace (2011); ~90 new users a month (2011), 30–50 a month (2012); a server crash damaged database files, restored from the administrator's backups (Oct 2012); a moderators' SOP covering personal-data requests (OVP, ~2010); weekly "This Week on CaveChat" posts on Caving News from 2012.
  • Companion sites run by the same sub-committee: OnlineCavers.com (Ning social network, ~1,000 members 2008, 2,532 in 2013, costing $599/yr) and the NSS Flickr group.
  • Sections (September 2008): General Discussion (10,610 posts), On Rope! (6,260), Equipment (6,028), Grotto Forums (4,006), Open Talk (3,677), NSS Topics (2,283); 20 sections in all.

  • The Board's reports stop carrying forum statistics after August 2013. From 2014 the forum appears only as one channel among several ("FB, Twitter, Cave Chat, and TAG-Net", AVP 2014; social-media managers posting "the applicable forum on CaveChat", PRES 2014).
  • 2015: a thread about grotto newsletters appearing on KIP, "focused on the possibility that cave locations could have been disclosed", leads KIP to pull all grotto newsletters (OVP-1503.pdf p.6).
  • 2015–2017 the IT Division lists "CaveChat online forum", with its administrator, among its activities. June 2017: the OVP is "looking for a webmaster and someone to take on CaveChat"; the directory lists the post vacant. 2018–2019: a new administrator listed; vacant again from 2020 onward.
  • August 2017: an outage when a caves.org firewall change exposed that the CaveChat web server was reaching its database by public address (OVP-1710.pdf p.8). October 2017: the Social Media Committee is deciding "which NSS team will moderate content CaveChat" (PRES-1710.pdf).
  • Membership kept rising (6,967 in 2015 to 8,013 in 2020, ~200 a year) while posting fell to a few hundred a year. On an unmoderated phpBB, late-period registrations are mostly automated accounts; nothing here identifies any.

5. Retirement on paper (2021)

  • 3–4 January 2021: 334,000 captures in two days, the whole public forum. 2021 accounts for 381,542 of the index's 782,179 rows.
  • March 2021 agenda (Agenda-2103.pdf p.4): "Cavechat.org has been unmaintained and unmanaged for some time. Utilization of the platform is low, with some forums not receiving more than one or two posts per year. While Cavechat still functions we will archive and preserve the contents of the site for future members while looking to more appropriate community-based discussion solutions for the 2020s. The NSS Discussion forum hosted at cavechat.org shall be archived by the IT Division and retired. The archive shall be made available to NSS members."
  • March 2021 minutes (2103BOG.pdf p.3, item 10): "Rescind Act 66-764, NSS Discussion Forum. Urbanski moved … Passed in Consent Agenda. PASSED." Item 9 at the same meeting required all committee websites to reside on IT-Division-managed servers within caves.org.
  • President's report, same meeting (PRES-2103.pdf p.18): the NSS Community Facebook group "has essentially fulfilled the role that CaveChat previously did in allowing personal and individual expression of ideas or opinions by members."
  • The November 2021 front page shows an announcements thread titled "The forum is back up again!", so there was at least one outage around this time.
  • Afterwards: the BOG Manual editions of June 2022 and October 2023 still print the NSS Forum Sub-Committee policy (administrator reports to the OVP; "currently phpBB"; backups; promotion of the forum). The OVP directory lists "Vacant, Cave Chat admin@cavechat.org" through March 2024. The Code of Conduct names "Cavechat.org" among NSS internal communities. The 2026 Youth Group Coordinator guidelines still tell grottos to link to "the Youth Groups section on the NSS discussion board".

6. Afterlife and end (2021–2025)

  • The forum stayed online and public. Front-page totals: 8,252 members / 128,533 posts (Nov 2021); 8,409 / 128,563 (Jul 2022); 8,682 / 128,578 (Feb 2023); 8,722 / 128,615 (Aug 2024); 8,732 / 128,639 (Jan 2025).
  • The Archive crawled it steadily (106,000 captures in 2022; 136,000 in 2024, about 14,000 a month).
  • Sections at the last capture (25 January 2025), posts and date of last post: General Discussion 21,985 (2024-11-06) · On Rope! 14,787 (2024-06-21) · Equipment 13,875 (2024-05-19) · Everything Bats 10,952 (2024-06-21) · Open Talk 5,396 (2019-01-11) · Survey and Cartography 4,759 (2024-06-24) · Photography 4,178 (2024-11-28) · Caving in the News 3,991 (2023-07-21) · Caver Events 3,400 (2021-07-30) · Caver Introductions 3,282 (2024-07-30) · Speleo-Marketplace 3,200 (2024-03-01) · Cave Rescue Techniques 2,739 (2020-05-02) · Trips & Expeditions 2,679 (2022-06-22) · Conservation 2,671 (2021-03-25) · Publications 2,316 (2024-11-05) · Speleology 2,176 (2024-10-29) · Cave Diving 1,238 (2024-03-01) · Joke Forum 940 (2018-01-27) · BORC 734 (2019-12-13) · Youth Groups 699 (2019-05-09) · Please Read 332 (2022-08-20) · NSS Announcements 232 (2015-08-20) · In Memory of 157 (2020-07-30) · Communications & Electronics Section 109 (2024-12-02) · NSS Archives 54 (2011-01-10) · Safety and Techniques 37 (2017-05-22) · NSS Grotto Forums 19 (2015-10-11).   • Last capture of a working forum: 28 March 2025 16:53 UTC. No public NSS document found so far records the shutdown or its cause.

9. What the public record does not say

  1. Why the forum went offline in late March 2025, and whether anyone decided it.
  2. Whether the IT Division produced the members' archive promised in March 2021, and where it is.
  3. Who holds cavechat.org after 28 September 2026, and whether the NSS still controls the name.
  4. Who made the 3–4 January 2021 crawl.
  5. Whether the forum's database survives on NSS equipment.

Method and limits

  • Index and front pages were retrieved by python -m crawler cc-discover / cc-robots / cc-fetch under the identity and limits in CRAWL_POLICY.md §6 (read-only, 1 request / 2 s, robots.txt honored, every request logged). 782,179 index rows, 30 robots.txt versions and 24 front-page captures were requested; nothing from the live site.
  • No topic, profile, member-list or search page was requested. The "newest member" field phpBB prints on its front page was blanked in data/cavechat_manifest.csv before this account was written.
  • Front-page totals are phpBB's own counters, which count all posts including those later deleted or in sections not shown to visitors; small differences from the Board's figures are expected.
  • Board-report passages are quoted from the extracted text in index/minutes.sqlite; file names are given so each can be checked against the PDF.
