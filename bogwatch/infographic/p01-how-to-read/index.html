What this is

An independent reading of the public record of the National Speleological Society: Board of Governors minutes, agendas and reports, the Board Manual in its successive editions, budgets, and the Form 990 returns filed with the IRS. It was compiled by Aaron Bird, NSS Fellow, NSS #27029. It is not an official NSS document.

Ground rules

  • Public sources only. Nothing here was obtained with a login. Members-only material, such as NSS News, was not used, and where that leaves a question open the page says "not checked".
  • Descriptive. Each page sets documents side by side and says whether they are consistent. It does not decide whether any rule was breached; that is a legal question these pages do not answer.
  • Traceable. Every panel names its source files, and every page links them.
  • Names. People are named as the records print them. In the who-was-in-office panel, names are withheld.

How to read a panel

The heading states the finding. The sentence under it is the takeaway. Cards, cells and tables carry the quotations, dates and page numbers. Colour never carries meaning alone: every coloured cell also has a word in it.

Why something is called an issue

CodeBasis
AThe record is not consistent with the Constitution, the Bylaws or an Act
BTwo of the Society's governing documents are not consistent with each other
CA statement by the Society is not consistent with another public record
DAn outside filing requirement, such as the annual Form 990
ESomething the documents say should exist is not in the public record

Terms

  • Fiscal year: ends 31 March. FY2022 runs from April 2021 to March 2022. (Before 2007 it ended 30 April.)
  • NSS: the National Speleological Society, EIN 54-6026867. NSF: the National Speleological Foundation, EIN 23-7282767, a separate corporation that holds funds for the Society.
  • Manual: the Board of Governors Manual, which prints the Constitution, Bylaws, Acts and appendices.
  • S-T: Secretary-Treasurer. ED: Executive Director.
