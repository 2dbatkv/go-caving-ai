Article III: the wording changed in 2024, and no member ballot is recorded

Article VIII says the Constitution is amended by a two-thirds vote of members’ ballots. The Board’s 2024 motion names only “bylaws and acts”.

The question

Was the 2024 change to Article III of the Constitution put to a member ballot, as Article VIII requires? This page reports what the public record contains and what it does not. It does not assert that no vote took place.

Compared word by word, this is the only change to the nine articles between the June 2023 and July 2024 Manuals.

The rule: Article VIII

An amendment is proposed by the Board or by a petition of five percent of regular or higher members; it is mailed "to all regular or higher members of the Society"; and it is adopted "by a two-thirds favorable vote of all ballots returned within ninety days of the mailing." The wording is the same in every public copy from 2008 to 2026. It has no exception for small or clerical changes.

What the 2024 record shows

  • Found: the Board adopted the restructuring on 10 March 2024, 16–0. The motion amends "bylaws and acts (including appendices)". It does not name the Constitution. (2403BOG.pdf, item 5.5)
  • Found: a floor amendment allowed "clerical changes" replacing "president" with "Executive Director" in three named Acts (18-640, 18-747, 18-986).
  • Not found: a Constitution section in the 144-page restructuring proposal.
  • Not found: the word "Constitution" in any public Board minutes or agenda from June 2023 to July 2026, apart from the text of one fund Act.
  • Not found: a ballot notice, a vote count, or a Manual entry for Article III.
  • Not checked: NSS News and election notices, which are members-only and are where a ballot would be announced.

Why it is an issue

Basis A (governing documents): the change is not consistent with Article VIII unless a ballot was held. Basis E (gap in the record): the trail that exists for the 2014 amendment (motion, notice, count, minute, Manual entry) is not in the public record for this one.

What would settle it

The ballot notice and the count, if there was a ballot. Members who voted in the 2024 or 2025 director elections, or who keep their NSS News, may be able to point to them.
