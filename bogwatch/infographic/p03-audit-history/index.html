3. What the Form 990s say (Part XII, lines 2a–2b)

Every NSS return in XML form, FY2018 through FY2025, answers that the financial statements were not compiled, not reviewed and not audited. The Foundation's returns for FY2019–FY2025 answer that its statements were audited and that it has an audit committee.

4. Summary

  1. The last audit report in the public record is for FY2008-09 (unqualified), by Randall L. Hunt, L.L.C.
  2. Two earlier audits were qualified (FY2006-07, FY2007-08), and the Society was dropped from the Combined Federal Campaign as a result.
  3. The Board suspended audits for FY2015-16, FY2016-17 and FY2017-18 by vote, each time with dissent.
  4. In November 2019 the standing requirement was reduced from an annual audit to an annual review.
  5. Act 15-510 still requires an annual CPA review, approved by the Board. No review report appears in the public record, and

the returns say none was done, for any year from FY2018 to FY2025.

  1. A full audit was contracted in November 2021. Nothing public says it was completed, what it found, or how it ended.
