What was public, by fiscal year

From about 2001 to 2010 the Society posted audited statements, year-end statements, a financial policy and budgets on a public "fiscal-information" page (now only in the Internet Archive). After that the public record is the officer reports attached to Board meetings, some budgets, and the Form 990. An annual financial report in NSS News is required by an Act of the Board, and NSS News is members-only, so it was not assessed.

Is the annual financial report in NSS News required?

Yes, by Act 15-181, "Financial Report", adopted 24 August 1968 and in force in the Manual of 6 July 2026. It is an Act, not a Bylaw.

EditionText
Manuals of 2022 and 2023"To keep the general membership informed of the financial state of the NSS, of its income and outgo, of the uses made of the expenditures, and of reasonable future expectations, the Secretary-Treasurer will write an annual informative report of the financial position of the NSS to be published in the NSS NEWS."

Sources: Manual, June 2023, p38; Manual, July 2026, p18.

In the July 2026 Manual the duty still rests with the Executive Director, a post that has been vacant since the contract was terminated on 29 June 2026. The related Act 15-776, the financial report to Board meetings, was moved to the Secretary-Treasurer in July 2026; Act 15-181 was not. Whether the annual report has appeared in NSS News in any given year was not checked, because NSS News is members-only.

Budget and Form 990

The budget the Board approves covers the operating fund; the Form 990 covers the whole corporation. In the years where both are public (FY2014, 2019, 2020, 2024, 2025) Form 990 expenses are 1.9 to 2.9 times the budget. That difference in scope is expected. No public document reconciles the two. The budgets for FY2026 and FY2027 show expenses of $1.12M and $1.52M, against $0.64M in FY2025.

The Foundation

The National Speleological Foundation reports total assets of $8.13M for FY2025, of which $2.22M is its own net assets; the rest is largely funds held for the NSS. Its returns are filed each year and report an audit, an audit committee, and written conflict-of-interest, whistleblower and retention policies in every year from FY2019.

Points a CPA should look at

  • FY2023: contributions of $2.69M alongside "other revenue" of −$813,979.
  • FY2021: revenue of $2.38M against expenses of $0.45M.
  • The FY2025 return's beginning net assets differ from FY2024's year-end figure by $8,605.
