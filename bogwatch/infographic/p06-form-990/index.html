People on the return and people on the roll

Spellings are as printed on the returns.

  • FY2024 (18 listed) against the roll of 10 March 2024 (17):

  • On the return, not on the roll (8): Gary Schnidel, Adam Weaver, Nick Anderson, Julie Brown, Riley Drake, Sonia Meyer, Doug Warner, Meredity Hall Webreg. Two people are listed as President.
  • FY2025 (12 listed) against the rolls of 30 September 2024 and 31 March 2025 (14 each):

  • On the return, on neither roll (3): Josh Schultz, Michael Mansur, Sarah Arpin.

A roll call is a single meeting and a return covers a year, so some difference is expected. The twelve names on the FY2025 return match the Board as it stood when the return was signed in October 2025.

Why it is an issue

Basis C (statement versus record): answers given to the IRS are not consistent with the Society's own Constitution, Bylaws, Acts, posted minutes and roll calls. These are comparisons of documents; a CPA should say whether any of it matters for filing purposes.
