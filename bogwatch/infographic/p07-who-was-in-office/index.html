How to read it

  • Solid mark: the public record shows the person held a Board seat or Society office when the issue arose.
  • Ringed mark: the person took office later, while the issue continued.
  • Dash: not in office at any point in that period.

A mark records presence in office. It does not record a vote, knowledge or responsibility. Names withheld. Each column is one person; the numbers carry no meaning beyond telling the columns apart.

How the cells were decided

Dates in office come from the roll calls in the Board’s posted minutes, 2018 to 2026: the first roll on which a person appears as a director, or the minute recording an office. Proxy appearances are not counted. Each issue has a dated period: when it arose, and whether it continues today. Two issues recur every year (no audit or review; minutes posted late), so each year in office counts as “arose”.
