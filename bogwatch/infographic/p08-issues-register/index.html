Detail: M8 — "No review was or will be conducted."

The form asks two things. Line 11a: was a complete copy of the Form 990 provided to all members of the governing body before filing? Line 11b: describe the process, if any, used to review the return.

Fiscal year11a: copy to every board member before filing11b: process described on Schedule O
FY2018, FY2019, FY2020, FY2021No"THE 990 IS REVIEWED BY THE SECRETARY/TREASURER AND THE SECRETARY/TREASURER DEPARTMENT INCLUDING THE FINANCE COMMITTEE"
FY2022---- (no return)
FY2023, FY2024, FY2025No"No review was or will be conducted."
Foundation (NSF), FY2019–FY2025, for comparisonYes"REVIEWED BY ALL BOARD MEMBERS"
  • Not one time: the sentence appears on three consecutive returns, prepared in two different years (two signed in July 2024 with a paid preparer, one in October 2025 with none named).
  • Not NSS's earlier practice: through FY2021 the returns described a review, though by the Secretary-Treasurer's own department and not by the Board. In no year from FY2018 did the NSS report giving the return to the whole Board before filing.
  • Is it standard? Board review of the 990 is not a legal requirement; the IRS asks the question so the public can see the answer. I have not sourced a sector-wide figure and would not put one on a slide without it. What the record does support is the direct comparison: the Foundation, which holds NSS funds, answers Yes to line 11a and "reviewed by all board members" every year.
  • Related: S-T reports for Jul 2021 say the FY2020 return (tax-year label "2019") was "attached" to the report, i.e. shown to the Board after filing. Minutes 2021–2026 never mention a 990.
  • Not checked: FY2015–17 returns (PDF only; Part VI needs reading by eye).

Detail: M15 — "about $80k in checks that have NOT been recorded"

Source: "Notes to Financial Statement 9-30-2023", attached to the Secretary-Treasurer's report for the November 2023 Board meeting, pp10–11. They end: "Treasurer - David Oase is providing these financials to the board and officers as a volunteer of the NSS. Not as a CPA in the practice of public account. - As you can see our accounting and bookkeeping functions are pretty far out of whack."

The full sentence: "Convention account has about $80k in checks that have NOT been recorded. In addition the final balance needs to be moved to the NSF Holding account." That is all the note says. It does not say which convention, whether the checks were written by or to the convention, or over what period.

Other items in the same notes:

  • "Deposits of $43k have been recorded as sales instead of dues and donations."
  • Salons checking "has not been updated since Nov of 2022"; Salons PayPal "has about 594 transactions that need to be recorded".
  • Restricted funds (Bat Research, Cave Acquisition, Rescue Training, Save the Cave): "we don't know the balances"; the spreadsheets are not up to date or reconciled. Same for NSF restricted funds.
  • Investment accounts "have not been updated since 3-31-23".
  • Venue rental deposits: "$30k in refunds paid to renters but none of the deposits from renters have been put in this account. Meaning at least $30k in Rental Income is NOT income."
  • "Deferred Revenue of $75k is probably NOT real."
  • "Convention Clearing account has been deleted. HQ Clearing account has been deleted."
  • Inventory at 31 Mar 2023: "This number is NOT real."
  • "Depreciation has NOT been recorded since 1-1-21."
  • "Main checking deposit of about $20k from 2022 never cleared the bank."
  • NCRC transactions are in a separate QuickBooks file "and only entered in these books once a year"; convention books are also separate and "may not agree".

What followed in the record:

  • Mar 2024 (notes dated 31 Dec 2023): "Our new bookkeeper has done a great job getting us caught up. The mistakes by the prior bookkeeper have been cleaned up." The $80k line is gone, with no statement of how it was resolved. Still listed: NCRC "$200k in revenue and expenses are NOT reflected in our books"; depreciation not recorded since 1 Jan 2021; inventory "not accurate".
  • Jul 2024 (notes dated 31 Mar 2024): "The books are in good shape … Currently only 1 account hasn't been reconciled."
  • Nov 2024 (new Finance lead): "Reconcile 2023 NSS Convention - This was previously reported to be closed but the QBO account has not been reconciled since April 2023. I have requested the statements from the bank because the bank account has been closed." Also: PayPal "hadn't been done in ~2.5 years, there was ~$50,000 that needed to be transferred".

Timing against the returns: the FY2023 return (year to 31 Mar 2023) and the FY2024 return (year to 31 Mar 2024) were both signed in July 2024, by the firm of the Treasurer who wrote these notes.
