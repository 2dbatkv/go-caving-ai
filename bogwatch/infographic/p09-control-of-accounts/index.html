What changed

At the 263rd Regular Meeting (6 and 10 July 2026), items 8.7 to 8.64 were listed as a consent agenda. The draft minutes record one second and one unanimous voice vote, placed after item 8.64. The meeting followed by one week the 29 June vote to "terminate the Executive Director's contract without cause."

In the same vote

  • 22 Acts in Sections 17–18 (funds) deleted; the remaining restricted funds placed in a Foundation "Holding Fund".
  • Act 15-617: the capital-purchase threshold needing Board approval raised from $500 to $1,500, and the tier the Executive Director could approve removed.
  • Act 15-885: decisions on facility surpluses moved to the Facilities Division.
  • Act 18-998, the Capital Expenses Fund created on 1 March 2025, rescinded.

Why it is an issue

Basis B (two governing documents): the Acts now give these duties to a Secretary-Treasurer, an office the Bylaws have not contained since 1 July 2024. Since September 2026 one person reports as "Secretary-Treasurer and Interim Executive Director" (report of 17 Sep 2026). Under Act 15-509 the reviewers' access to bank accounts is arranged by the officer whose accounts are reviewed.

What the record does not show

The reasons for the changes are not stated in the minutes. Only draft minutes of the meeting are public.
