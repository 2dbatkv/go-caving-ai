How they were collected

Files were retrieved between 22 and 30 September 2026 by a client that identifies its operator in every request, uses only read requests, never logs in, keeps no cookies, honours robots.txt and is rate-limited. Every request is logged. For each file the project keeps the address, the retrieval time and a SHA-256 fingerprint, so anyone can confirm that a copy matches.

What was not used

  • NSS News and other members-only material.
  • Anything behind a login, including the Society's legacy site and the Board's internal storage.
  • Paid or sign-up databases.

Check it yourself

  • Read the returns at the IRS link above.
  • IRS Form 4506-A requests a copy of a named year's Form 990; the IRS sends it or reports that none is on file.
  • Anyone may ask an exempt organisation in writing for its three most recent returns (IRC §6104(d)).
