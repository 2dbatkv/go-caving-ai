BOG Watch · Issue M4 · Audit and books

No audit or review report since FY2008-09, although an Act has required one every year

Which Act, whether it is in force, how it has changed since 1993, what the record shows against it, and what would settle the question.

Not affiliated with the NSS. Every document referred to here was retrieved from a public web address, with no login, no account and no cookies. These pages set documents side by side and say where they are not consistent. They are not legal or accounting advice.

1. The claim

The Society's own Acts have required an outside certified public accountant to audit (1993–2019) or review (2019–today) the books every year. The last such report in the public record is the audit for the fiscal year ended 31 March 2009. Every Form 990 from FY2018 to FY2025 states that the financial statements were not audited, not reviewed and not compiled.

3. The rule and its history

Which Act? Act 15-510, in Section 15 (Fiscal) of the Board of Governors Manual. Related: Act 15-700 (contracts for audit/review must have a fixed cost ceiling), Act 15-770 (Audit, later Review, Committee Charter) and Appendix AV (the committee's policy).

Is it in force now? Yes. It is printed in the Manual "As of the July 06, 2026 meeting", p19.

Has it been in force all along? The Act has been in the Manual since 1993. What it requires has changed five times:

Times the Board set the Act aside:

  • 18 Mar 2017: "Not withstanding Act 15-510 the NSS will not conduct audits for FY 15/16 or FY 16/17." Against: Harler. (1703BOG p5)
  • 16 Mar 2019: "Audit of FY 17/18 is suspended." A review "in lieu of an independent audit" for FY2018-19; "Regular independent audits shall resume for FY 19/20." Against: Crispin, Skove, DeVries, Speaect. (1903BOG p4)

A wording point inside the Act: since 2019 the text asks for a "review … in conformity with auditing standards … (US GAAP audit)". The word "audit" was replaced in some places and left in others.

5. What the record does not show

  • Any audit or review report after FY2008-09, or a statement that one was completed.
  • How the 2021 engagement ended, what was paid, or what the auditor found.
  • Whether a review was done for FY2018-19 as the Board ordered.
  • A Board vote approving reviewed statements for any year since 2019, as the Act requires.
  • FY2010 to FY2015: not yet traced in the Secretary-Treasurer reports.
  • Not checked: members-only material (NSS News; the Board's internal storage).

6. Other explanations to keep in mind

  • Reports may exist and simply not be posted. Posting is not required by the Act.
  • A review may have been done in some year and the Form 990 box left unchecked in error.
  • The Board may have treated the 2021 audit contract as satisfying the review requirement while it was under way.
  • No law requires an organisation of this kind to be audited; the 2019 agenda note says so, and that was the stated reason for the change.
  • The suspensions of 2017 and 2019 were taken by recorded vote in open session.

7. Who was in office

See panel P07, row M4. The requirement recurs every year, so everyone in office since 15 Nov 2021 is marked. A mark records presence in office; it does not record a vote, knowledge or responsibility.

8. What would settle it

  1. The Borland and Benefield engagement letter and any report, or a statement that the engagement ended without one.
  2. Any review report for FY2019 to FY2025, with the Board minute approving it.
  3. The Financial Review Committee's reports to the Board.
