A short list, kept by hand. You will hear when a call is coming up and when something new goes on this site — nothing else.

  • Your address is never shown. No names or addresses appear anywhere on this site, and messages are sent so that recipients cannot see one another.
  • It is never shared or sold, and never used for anything but BOG Watch calls and postings.
  • Leaving is easy. Reply to any message and say so, and you come off the list straight away.
  • Only what you type here is collected — an email address, and a name if you choose to give one.
