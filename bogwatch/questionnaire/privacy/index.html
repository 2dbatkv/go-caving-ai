Privacy notice

How the answers to “What do cavers want from the organizations that represent them?” are collected, kept and deleted.

Who is responsible

BOG Watch, an independent project run by Aaron Bird. It is not run by, or affiliated with, any caving organization. Contact: gocavingai@gmail.com.

What is collected

  • Your answers. Every question is optional.
  • The date and time you sent them.
  • Three hidden fields: the currency you chose to see amounts in; which link you used (for example, a club email), so we can show how the questionnaire was shared; and a version number for the form.

What is not collected

  • Your name or email address.
  • Your IP address or location. [To confirm before launch: the Tally setting that keeps IP addresses out of the responses.]
  • Anything that links your answers to you. Where you live is only what you choose to tell us.

Repeat submissions

The form allows one response per browser. This does not identify you. [To confirm before launch: how Tally does this, and whether its bot check processes IP addresses.]

Who handles the data

The form is hosted by Tally (tally.so), under its own privacy policy. No one else receives individual responses. Individual responses are never sold, shared or published.

The questionnaire page on gocaving.ai loads the form, and a small script that sizes it, directly from Tally’s site, so your browser connects to Tally when you open that page.

How long it is kept

  • On Tally

    Until the questionnaire closes (not before 15 January 2027). Within 14 days of the close, the answers are exported and deleted from Tally.

  • Exported answers

    Kept by BOG Watch until 31 December 2028, so the published results can be checked, then deleted.

  • Published results

    Totals and percentages only, kept online. Groups with fewer than 30 responses are combined with others. Written answers may be quoted with anything identifying removed.

  • Notification list

    A separate, optional form. Your email is never stored with your answers. It is kept until you leave the list (reply to any message), and deleted if BOG Watch ends.

Your choices

You can skip any question, or stop at any time before sending, and nothing is recorded. Because answers are anonymous, a response cannot be found or removed after it is sent. You can leave the notification list at any time.

Age

The questionnaire is for people 13 or older. If you are 13 to 17, please check with a parent or guardian before taking part. The notification list is for adults (18 or older) only.

Changes

If this notice changes while the questionnaire is open, the new version and its date will be posted here.
