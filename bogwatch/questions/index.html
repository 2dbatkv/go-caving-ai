Questions for the Board

70 questions for the National Speleological Society Board of Governors, for the 264th Board meeting on Saturday 17 October 2026 and the member Town Hall. Each one says why it is asked and links the public documents it rests on.

Questions, not findings. Where two public records are not consistent, the question asks which is right and why. Every link goes to a document the Society or the IRS has published, or to a BOG Watch page that links them. Link to any question by its number, for example gocaving.ai/bogwatch/questions/#q15.
