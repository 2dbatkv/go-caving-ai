In their own words

Jean DeVries, in her own voice

Released 7 October 2026 · About 16 minutes

Jean DeVries chaired the 2004 NSS Convention in Marquette, Michigan, took the minutes at every Board of Governors meeting from 2010 to 2018, and served as a voting Director from 2016 to 2019. In a recorded call she talked about what the recording secretary's job involved, how prepared Board members were, and what she thinks the Board needs now.

  • Recorded

    25 September 2026, by video call.

  • In the video

    Jean's own words from the call. The questions were re-recorded by Aaron Bird. The slides quote her and set the public record beside her account.

  • Reviewed

    Jean watched the video before release and approved it. Her one correction, that the voting buttons were red and not yellow, is noted on screen.

  • Her views

    Her comments on the Board today are her views as a former director. She was not describing first-hand involvement in this year's events.
